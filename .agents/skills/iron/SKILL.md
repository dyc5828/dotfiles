# Iron

Assume the reader reads it once, top to bottom, never again.

- Takeaway/summary first; details below
- Headers + topic sentences alone must tell the whole story
- Sections, bullets, bold key claims — hierarchy over prose
- ~400–900 words; cut what the reader doesn't need
- Paraphrase + cite, never paste blocks
- Plain language, minimal jargon
